What Wardlight does

Wardlight analyzes publicly available job-posting information and user-provided text for documented patterns. Its results are informational assessments, not statements of fact about an employer or person.

No guarantee

Results can be incomplete or wrong. Wardlight does not guarantee a job posting is legitimate, fraudulent, open, closed, or likely to lead to employment. Use your own judgment before sharing information, paying fees, or applying.
