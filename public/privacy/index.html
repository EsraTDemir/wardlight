PRIVACY NOTICE

We collect as little as possible.

Last updated: August 9, 2026.

What stays in your browser

Text pasted into Wardlight's free analyzer is evaluated locally in your browser. Wardlight does not send or store that pasted text.

Pilot requests

If you submit the pilot form, Wardlight collects your organization, work email, organization type, selected interest, and optional message. We use this information only to respond to your request and improve the pilot program. Submissions are processed through Netlify Forms.

Job posting data

Wardlight and GhostWatch observe publicly available job posting information. The public website shows aggregate counts, not a list of individual submissions from visitors.

Retention and choices

You can ask us to delete a pilot request by submitting a new request with "delete my request" in the message field. We do not sell personal information or use advertising trackers.
