EXHIBIT A — THE PROBLEM

Some job postings are never meant to be filled.

Independent estimates cited by state regulators put ghost listings at one-fifth to one-third of online job postings. They waste your applications, your evenings, and your confidence — and from a single glance, they look exactly like real jobs. The tell isn't in the posting. It's in the posting's behavior over time.

EXHIBIT B — THE METHOD

Ghosts can't survive a diary.

Observe daily

We watch postings across job boards every day and record what we see — an independent timestamped diary no employer or platform can edit.

Detect behavior

Repost cycles under new IDs. "Posted" dates younger than our own first sighting. Listings open far past normal hiring timelines. Scam signatures — fee requests, off-platform interviews, lookalike domains. Documented rules, no vibes.

Verdict, with receipts

Each posting gets a score and a plain-English evidence trail. Verdicts describe posting behavior — patterns, not accusations — so you can judge the evidence yourself.

Scam warnings are free. Forever. Verdicts that protect you from identity theft and fee fraud will never sit behind a paywall — at any tier, under any future pricing. That's a commitment, not a promo.
